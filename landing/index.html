The honest bit

Yes, another AI productivity thing.

The internet is full of beautiful systems for becoming the person who finally gets things done.

Most of them are productivity porn: a satisfying setup, a fresh dashboard, and no finished work on Friday. We know the genre because we have bought the notebooks too.

Try Swingshift if you want the unglamorous part. A team that keeps going after the demo, checks its own work, leaves a trail, and stops when the goal is actually met.

The loop

Give the work a second shift.

01 / BRIEF

Say what good looks like.

A rough goal is enough. The manager breaks it into work the team can actually check.

02 / HAND OFF

Agents read each other.

Builders build, reviewers review, and the next person gets the context instead of a blank chat.

03 / RESOLVE

Stop with evidence.

The loop ends when the target is met or asks you for a decision. You get the result and the trail.

Questions we get
Does it replace my agent?

No. It gives the agents you already use a team structure and a finish line.

Do I have to move my code?

No. Swingshift is designed to run beside the projects and tools you already own.

Is this another cloud dashboard?

The beta is self-hosted. The control plane can be remote; the work and keys stay on your machine.

What happens after I sign up?

We’ll email you when a slot opens and send a short setup link. You can leave at any time.
